# Automation Ideas

## 1. Auto Email After Registration
When a student registers, Salesforce automatically sends a confirmation email.

### Why Automation Helps
- Saves time
- Faster communication
- Reduces manual work

---

## 2. Auto Update Remaining Seats
When a student joins a course, available seats update automatically.

### Why Automation Helps
- Prevents overbooking
- Keeps data accurate
- Faster management

---

## 3. Notify Faculty When Course is Full
Salesforce sends notification to faculty when all seats are filled.

### Why Automation Helps
- Quick updates
- Better course management
- Saves staff effort

---

## 4. Generate Student ID Automatically
System automatically creates a student ID after registration.

### Why Automation Helps
- Reduces errors
- Faster process
- Unique identification

---

## 5. Send Fee Deadline Reminder
Students automatically receive reminders before the fee deadline.

### Why Automation Helps
- Improves payment tracking
- Reduces missed deadlines
- Saves administrative effort
